# wyf9sBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-10-31**  
**Last updated: 2026-10-31**

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies only to the official **wyf9sBot** (the "Service") operated by **wyf9, an individual developer** (the "Operator") through the Discord application.

It does **not** apply to any third party that self-hosts or operates its own wyf9sBot instance. Such operators are independent data controllers and must provide their own privacy information and bear their own responsibilities.

## 2. Data We Process

To provide its features, the Service processes:

| Data | Examples | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discord identifiers | User ID, username, server ID, channel / thread ID, role ID | Permission checks, list matching, lock schedules, announcement subscription, audit records, language preference |
| Command input and message content | Command arguments, messages being cleaned, messages evaluated by anti-spam | Executing commands, bulk cleanup, anti-spam actions, and automatic replies |
| Configuration and runtime data | Language settings, dynamic permissions, scheduled locks, voice sessions, chat enhance toggles | Persisting server / user preferences and keeping features working |
| Logs and audit records | Command invocations, errors, moderation actions, automated action results | Debugging, auditing, security, and abuse prevention |

The Service does not process payment information and does not ask you for passwords.

## 3. Purposes

Data is used only to:

- provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
- carry out operations initiated by you or your server administrators;
- perform permission checks, rate limiting, anti-spam, and auditing;
- diagnose issues, secure the Service, and prevent abuse;
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Storage and Retention

Data is primarily stored in local files on the server the Operator uses to run the Service, and is transmitted and displayed via the Discord platform.

- Configuration, permissions, scheduled locks, language preferences, and similar data are retained until deleted, overwritten, or the related feature is no longer used.
- Logs rotate and are retained according to the runtime configuration (about 3 days by default, as configured).
- Audit logs, anti-spam notices, and similar records exist as Discord messages in the relevant channels, and their retention is affected by Discord and channel settings.
- Message content is generally read only during processing; retention may occur only for auditing, anti-spam forwarding, automatic replies, or when you explicitly ask for something to be saved.

The Operator retains data for as long as needed for the purposes above and deletes or anonymizes it when no longer needed.

## 5. Sharing and Processors

The Operator does **not sell** your personal data.

Data may be shared with or processed by third parties in these cases:

- **Discord**: the Service runs through the Discord API, and related data is subject to Discord's privacy policy;
- **Hosting providers**: servers or cloud providers the Operator uses to deploy and run the Service, processing data only as needed to provide infrastructure;
- **Third-party feature services**: if you use emoji or tweet conversion features, requests are sent to the configured third-party services and may include technical information needed to serve the request (such as IP address and request parameters), but are not used to identify or track you;
- **Legal requirements**: where required by applicable law, or where necessary to protect the rights and interests of the Operator, users, or the public, the Operator may disclose relevant data.

## 6. Security

The Operator takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including access restrictions, controlled runtime environments, and periodic log review. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and the Operator cannot promise that data will never be breached, lost, or accessed without authorization.

## 7. Your Rights

Where permitted by applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data related to the Service, or object to / restrict certain processing.

You can:

- manage your language preference with `/lang`;
- remove users or roles from dynamic permission rules with `/perm rm`;
- remove scheduled locks with `/lock unplan`;
- ask your server administrators to remove your information from the server configuration;
- email **legal@wyf9.top** with data-related requests.

To protect data security, the Operator may need to verify your Discord account or the connection between the request and the data. Deleting some audit records or Discord messages may be limited by Discord platform mechanics and channel settings.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

The Service does not use cookies, does not perform advertising tracking, and does not build cross-server user profiles.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

The Operator may update this Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Material changes will be announced reasonably.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests: **legal@wyf9.top**.
